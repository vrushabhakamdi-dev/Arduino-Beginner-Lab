CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LED (any color)
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D13
K (-) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- D13 also drives the Uno's built-in LED, so the sketch runs even with no external parts.
- The 220 ohm resistor must stay in series with the LED or the LED can burn out.
- Open the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud to see the LED ON / LED OFF messages.
