CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

RAIN DROP SENSOR MODULE (with comparator board)
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
DO  → Arduino D2
  DO = comparator output, goes LOW the moment rain is detected
AO  → Arduino A0
  AO = raw wetness level 0..1023, falls as the plate gets wetter

PIEZO BUZZER
+   → Arduino D8   (through a 220 ohm resistor)
-   → Arduino GND

LED
+ (anode)   → Arduino D4   (through a 220 ohm resistor)
- (cathode) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- Two 220 ohm resistors are needed: one in series with the buzzer (+) and one with the LED anode. The sensor module needs no resistor.
- The sensing plate is bare metal: keep it well away from the Arduino board and dry the plate after use.
- The alarm triggers when DO reads LOW or the analog level drops to 500 or below (DRY_THRESHOLD in the sketch) - tune the module potentiometer and the threshold for your plate.
- This project is an alarm only: nothing here drives a pump or valve.
- Built on a breadboard with jumper wires; upload with the USB cable and watch the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
