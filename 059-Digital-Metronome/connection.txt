CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

PUSH BUTTON - TEMPO UP (+5 BPM)
One leg   → Arduino D2
Other leg → GND
  Each press adds 5 BPM (40 - 220 BPM).

PUSH BUTTON - TEMPO DOWN (-5 BPM)
One leg   → Arduino D3
Other leg → GND
  Each press removes 5 BPM.

PUSH BUTTON - START / STOP
One leg   → Arduino D4
Other leg → GND
  Starts or stops the beat.

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
- → GND
  Tick on every beat; higher pitch on beat 4 (accent).

BUILT-IN LED
Anode → Arduino D13 (already on the board)
  Flashes for 50 ms on every beat.

NOTES
- Buttons: internal pull-ups are enabled, so each button only shorts its pin
  to GND. No external resistors.
- tone() needs a PASSIVE buzzer - the metronome uses two pitches (1000 Hz and
  1800 Hz accent), which an active buzzer cannot produce.
- D8 is used for the buzzer because D2, D3 and D4 are taken by the buttons;
  the flash LED is the built-in one on D13, so no external LED is needed.
- Tempo and beat lines are printed in the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
