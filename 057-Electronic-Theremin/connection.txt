CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LDR (LIGHT DEPENDENT RESISTOR) - voltage divider
One leg       → Arduino 5V
Other leg     → Arduino A0
One leg of the 10k ohm resistor → Arduino A0
Other leg of the 10k ohm resistor → Arduino GND

  5V ──[ LDR ]──┬──[ 10k ohm ]── GND
                │
             Arduino A0

  A0 senses the middle of the divider: more light = higher voltage.

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
- → GND
  Plays the continuous 200 - 4000 Hz tone.

NOTES
- The LDR and the 10k resistor must meet at A0 - that junction is the divider
  output. Wiring the LDR straight to A0 with no resistor gives no reading.
- tone() needs a PASSIVE buzzer; an active buzzer plays only one fixed note
  and cannot follow your hand.
- Cover the LDR to lower the pitch, uncover it to raise it. Nothing else to
  play: no buttons in this build.
- The current frequency is printed in the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
