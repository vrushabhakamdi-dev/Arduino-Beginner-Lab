CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LDR (VOLTAGE DIVIDER)
one leg → Arduino 5V
other leg → Arduino A0     midpoint of the divider
10 kohm resistor → Arduino A0 to Arduino GND

   5V --- LDR ---+--- A0
                 |
              10 kohm
                 |
   GND ----------+

LED 1 (BAR LOW)
+ → Arduino D9
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

LED 2
+ → Arduino D10
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

LED 3
+ → Arduino D11
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

LED 4
+ → Arduino D12
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

LED 5 (BAR HIGH)
+ → Arduino D13
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

NOTES
- More light = lower LDR resistance = higher voltage at A0 = higher reading (0 = dark, 1023 = brightest).
- The 10 kohm resistor must sit between A0 and GND; putting it between 5V and A0 inverts the scale.
- D13 also drives the Uno's built-in LED, so LED 5 and the on-board LED light together.
- The bar refreshes every 200 ms and shows 0 to 5 LEDs lit.
