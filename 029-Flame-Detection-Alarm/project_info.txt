PROJECT NAME: 029 - Flame Detection Alarm
PROJECT IDEA: Detect the infrared light emitted by a flame with a KY-026 sensor and raise a flashing LED and buzzer alarm while the flame is present.
HOW IT WORKS: A flame gives off strong infrared radiation, and the KY-026's IR-sensitive photodiode turns that radiation into a change on its digital output pin. The Arduino reads pin 2, compares it against the level that means "flame" on that particular board, and switches on a fast-flashing buzzer and LED as soon as a match occurs. The Serial Monitor shows a FLAME DETECTED message at the moment of detection and a regular status line when the sensor is clear, which also helps you confirm whether your board is active-LOW or active-HIGH.
ADVANTAGES:
- Fast, non-contact detection of a fire's infrared signature.
- Digital output means no library and no analogue calibration to worry about.
- Serial status lines let a beginner verify wiring and polarity immediately.
- Flashing and beeping together alert people who cannot see the display.
- All timing is done with millis(), so the alarm reacts without lag.
REAL-WORLD USES:
- Early warning in kitchens, workshops and laboratories.
- Flame presence checks on burners, boilers and furnace pilot lights.
- Fire-severity hobby projects and science fair demonstrations.
- Monitoring candles, gas fires or model rocket test stands.
- Companion sensor alongside smoke detectors in a wider alarm system.
WHAT YOU LEARN:
- How infrared flame sensors detect a fire's emission spectrum.
- Why sensor polarity differs between boards and how to test for it.
- Using digitalRead() for alarm conditions with clear Serial feedback.
- Creating a non-blocking flashing pattern with millis().
- Writing the enter/exit logic that gives one-shot alarm messages.
FUTURE SCOPE: Combining this sensor with a smoke or gas sensor would allow a multi-input fire alarm with different alerts for different hazards.
POSSIBLE UPGRADES:
- Add a second flame sensor facing another direction for wider coverage.
- Latch the alarm so it keeps sounding until a reset button is pressed.
- Drive a relay to cut power or switch on an extractor fan.
- Send a timestamped alarm log over Serial for later review.
REQUIRED COMPONENTS:
- Arduino Uno
- KY-026 flame sensor module
- Piezo buzzer
- LED (the built-in LED on pin 13 can be used)
- 220 ohm resistor
- Breadboard
- Jumper wires
- USB cable
- Library: None
DIFFICULTY: Beginner
LIBRARY REQUIRED: None
