CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LED
Anode (+) → Arduino D13     (through 220 ohm resistor)
Cathode (−) → GND

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
− → GND

NOTES
- One 220 ohm resistor in series with the LED.
- Use a passive buzzer: tone() has to set its pitch itself.
- Powered from the USB cable only; breadboard and jumper wires complete the circuit.
