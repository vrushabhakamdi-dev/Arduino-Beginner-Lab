CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

FC-51 IR OBSTACLE SENSOR
VCC    → Arduino 5V
GND    → Arduino GND
OUT    → Arduino D2

PUMP DRIVER (NPN 2N2222 / TIP120 or logic-level NMOS)
base/gate → Arduino D6   (1k ohm resistor in series)
emitter   → Arduino GND
collector → pump (-)

LOW-VOLTAGE DC PUMP (5 V)
pump (+) → external supply (+)
pump (-) → transistor collector

FLYBACK DIODE (1N4007) across the pump
anode    → pump (-)
cathode  → pump (+)      (stripe/band to +V)

EXTERNAL PUMP SUPPLY (4 x AA or 5 V adapter)
+ → pump (+)
- → Arduino GND          (common ground is required)

LED (dispense indicator)
anode   (+) → Arduino D13   (220 ohm resistor in series)
cathode (-) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- Power the pump from its own supply, never from the Arduino 5V pin, and always share a common ground.
- Keep the reservoir, tubing and liquid well away from the Arduino, breadboard and USB cable.
- Code assumes the FC-51 OUT is active-low (OBSTACLE_ACTIVE_LOW = true).
- One hand in front of the sensor = one 1200 ms dose; remove the hand to re-arm.
