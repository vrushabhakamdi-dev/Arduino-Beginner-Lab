CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

SG90 MICRO SERVO
+ (red)          → Arduino 5V
- (brown/black)  → Arduino GND
S (orange)       → Arduino D9     PWM sweep signal

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE
no pin mapping - used for prototyping and power

NOTES
- The servo signal must be on D9, the sketch does myServo.attach(9).
- An SG90 can run from the Uno 5V pin; for a bigger servo use an external
  5V supply and join that supply GND to Arduino GND.
