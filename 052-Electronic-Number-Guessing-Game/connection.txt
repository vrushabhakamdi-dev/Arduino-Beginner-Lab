CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

BUILT-IN LED
Anode → Arduino D13 (already on the board)
  Blinks three times during the win celebration.

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
- → GND
  Plays the rising win tune.

A0 - RANDOM NOISE SEED
Nothing connected → leave Arduino A0 open
  The floating pin gives random noise for randomSeed().

NOTES
- The game is played by typing guesses in the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
  Send a number and Enter, or the letter r to restart. No buttons to wire.
- A0 is read on purpose with nothing attached - do NOT tie it to 5V or GND,
  otherwise every game would pick the same secret number.
- tone() needs a PASSIVE buzzer; an active buzzer cannot play the melody.
- Optional external LED: Anode → 220 ohm resistor → D13, Cathode → GND
  (only if you want a brighter light than the built-in LED).
