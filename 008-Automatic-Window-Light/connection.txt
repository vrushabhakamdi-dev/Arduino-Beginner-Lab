CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LDR + 10k RESISTOR (voltage divider)
5V → LDR → Arduino A0
Arduino A0 → 10k ohm resistor → Arduino GND

        5V ---[ LDR ]+--- A0
                     |
                  [ 10k ]
                     |
                   GND

LED
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D9
K (-) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- LDR must be on the 5V side and the 10k on the GND side: this way the A0
  reading rises with light (bright = high value, dark = low value).
- Swap them and the logic inverts, so the LED would switch on in daylight.
- Thresholds in the code: below 400 = dark (LED ON), above 600 = bright (LED OFF).
  Re-tune DARK_THRESHOLD / BRIGHT_THRESHOLD if your room lighting differs.
