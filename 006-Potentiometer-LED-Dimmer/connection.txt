CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

POTENTIOMETER (10k)
+ (outer leg) → Arduino 5V
S (wiper, middle leg) → Arduino A0
- (outer leg) → Arduino GND

        5V ---[  10k POT  ]--- GND
                  |
                 A0

LED
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D9
K (-) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- D9 is a PWM pin (marked with ~), which is what makes smooth dimming possible.
- If the LED only shows two brightness levels, check that the wiper really goes to A0.
