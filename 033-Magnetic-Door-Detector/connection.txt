CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

REED SWITCH
leg 1 → Arduino D2
  input with internal pull-up (INPUT_PULLUP) - no external resistor needed
leg 2 → Arduino GND
  switch closes when the magnet comes near, pulling D2 down to GND

SMALL MAGNET (door magnet set)
no wires - mount it on the door or window so it sits right beside the reed switch when closed

BUILT-IN LED (on the Arduino Uno)
LED → Arduino D13
  on-board indicator LED, ON while the door is OPEN

NOTES
- Active-LOW logic: magnet close = switch closed = D2 LOW = DOOR CLOSED; magnet away = D2 HIGH = DOOR OPEN.
- Do not add an external pull-up resistor: pin 2 already has one inside the chip, which is why the circuit is just switch to GND.
- A 50 ms debounce in the code waits out the reed contact bounce before accepting a new state.
- Use jumper wires (breadboard optional); upload with the USB cable and watch the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
