CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

FC-51 IR OBSTACLE SENSOR
VCC  → Arduino 5V
GND  → Arduino GND
OUT  → Arduino D2     active LOW = obstacle ("car") seen

SG90 MICRO SERVO
+ (red)         → Arduino 5V
- (brown/black) → Arduino GND
S (orange)      → Arduino D6     PWM barrier arm signal

5MM LED (WARNING LAMP)
+ (anode)   → Arduino D13   through 220 ohm resistor
- (cathode) → Arduino GND

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE
no pin mapping - used for prototyping and power

NOTES
- The FC-51 output is LOW when it detects an obstacle (code: OBSTACLE_DETECTED = LOW).
- D13 already has a built-in LED on the Uno, so the external LED and its
  220 ohm resistor are optional.
- Servo signal must be on D6, the sketch does barrier.attach(6).
