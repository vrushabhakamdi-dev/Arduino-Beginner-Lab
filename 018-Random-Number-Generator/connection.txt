CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

PUSH BUTTON
Leg 1 → Arduino D2
Leg 2 → GND

LED
Anode (+) → Arduino D13     (through 220 ohm resistor)
Cathode (−) → GND

RANDOM SEED PIN
A0 → not connected (leave floating)
  The floating pin picks up electrical noise and seeds random() at start-up.

NOTES
- Internal pull-up on the button: press reads LOW, no external resistor.
- One 220 ohm resistor in series with the LED.
- Breadboard, jumper wires and USB cable only; no external supply.
