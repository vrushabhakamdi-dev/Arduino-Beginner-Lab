CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

SIGNAL INPUT (0-5 V square wave)
signal → Arduino D2     INT0 - must be pin 2 (pin 3 would be INT1)
GND    → Arduino GND    signal source and Uno must share a ground

SIGNAL SOURCE (second Arduino tone(), function generator, 555, PWM)
output pin → Arduino D2
GND       → Arduino GND

OPTIONAL VOLTAGE DIVIDER - only for signals above 5 V:

  signal ---[R1]---+--- Arduino D2
                   |
                  [R2]
                   |
                  GND

  Example: R1 = 10k, R2 = 20k turns 12 V into 4 V.
  10k/10k only halves the voltage, so pick a ratio that keeps D2 at or
  below 5 V.

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE
no pin mapping - used for prototyping and power

NOTES
- D2 must never see less than 0 V or more than 5 V, and never AC: scale or
  clamp the signal first. The Uno pins are not 5 V-tolerant above 5 V.
- Frequency = pulses counted in the 1 s gate / elapsed seconds;
  period = 1 / frequency.
- No signal on D2 is reported as 0.00 Hz (no-signal), not as an error.
- No library required - attachInterrupt() does the counting.
