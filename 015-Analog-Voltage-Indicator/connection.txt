CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

POTENTIOMETER (10 kOhm) - test voltage
Outer pin 1 → 5V
Outer pin 2 → GND
Wiper (middle pin) → Arduino A0     (voltage being measured, 0-5 V)

THRESHOLD LEDs (5 LEDs, 220 ohm resistor each)
Anode (+) → Arduino D4     (lights at 1 V)
Anode (+) → Arduino D5     (lights at 2 V)
Anode (+) → Arduino D6     (lights at 3 V)
Anode (+) → Arduino D7     (lights at 4 V)
Anode (+) → Arduino D8     (lights at 5 V)
Cathode (−) → GND          (all five cathodes on the GND rail)

NOTES
- A0 must never see more than 5 V - keep the potentiometer between 5V and GND only.
- The two outer potentiometer pins can be swapped, the scale just reverses.
- One 220 ohm resistor per LED. USB cable powers the board.
- Breadboard and jumper wires for the rest.
