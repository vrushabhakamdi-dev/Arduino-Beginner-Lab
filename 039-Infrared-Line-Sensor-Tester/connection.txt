CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

TCRT5000 INFRARED REFLECTANCE SENSOR MODULE
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
AO  → Arduino A0
  AO = raw reflected-light value 0..1023, drops on a dark surface
DO  → Arduino D2
  DO = comparator output, LOW when a dark line is detected

BUILT-IN LED (on the Arduino Uno)
LED → Arduino D13
  on-board indicator LED, ON while a line is detected

NOTES
- Turn the module's on-board potentiometer until the printed LINE / NO LINE matches a black and a white surface held under the sensor; watch the raw= value in the Serial Monitor while tuning.
- Hold the sensor only a few millimetres above the surface; sunlight or other infrared sources will skew the readings.
- No external LED or resistor is required - the indicator is the built-in LED on D13.
- Use jumper wires; upload with the USB cable and watch the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
