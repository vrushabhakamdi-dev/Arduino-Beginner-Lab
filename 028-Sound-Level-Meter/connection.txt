CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

ANALOG SOUND SENSOR MODULE
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
AO  → Arduino A0    analog output - microphone level, 0 to 1023
DO  → not connected (digital output not used by this sketch)

LED (LOUD SOUND INDICATOR)
+ → Arduino D13
- → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino GND

NOTES
- Only AO is used. Leave DO open - its trimmer sets a compare threshold this sketch never reads.
- Turn the module's sensitivity trimmer to about the middle before testing.
- Pin 13 already has the Uno's built-in LED, so the external LED (and its 220 ohm resistor) is optional.
- The LED turns on at level 70 or above; the held peak decays after 1.5 seconds.
