CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

PUSH BUTTON - ROCK
One leg   → Arduino D2
Other leg → GND
  Throw rock.

PUSH BUTTON - PAPER
One leg   → Arduino D3
Other leg → GND
  Throw paper.

PUSH BUTTON - SCISSORS
One leg   → Arduino D4
Other leg → GND
  Throw scissors.

LED - WIN
Anode (+)   → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D5
Cathode (-) → GND
  On for 1.5 s when you win the round.

LED - LOSE
Anode (+)   → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D6
Cathode (-) → GND
  On for 1.5 s when the CPU wins the round.

LED - DRAW
Anode (+)   → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D7
Cathode (-) → GND
  On for 1.5 s when both moves are the same.

NOTES
- No buzzer in this build.
- Buttons: internal pull-ups are enabled, so each button only shorts its pin
  to GND. No external resistors.
- LED long leg = anode (+), always in series with a 220 ohm resistor.
- The battle log and running score are printed in the Serial Monitor at
  9600 baud.
