PROJECT NAME: 054 - Rock Paper Scissors Game
PROJECT IDEA: Play a best-of-five match of Rock Paper Scissors against the Arduino by pressing one of three buttons and watching the win, lose or draw LEDs.
HOW IT WORKS: Three buttons on pins 2, 3 and 4 select Rock, Paper or Scissors, and the Arduino randomly generates its own move with random(0, 3). The judging logic compares the two moves, adds a point to the correct score and lights the win, lose or draw LED on pins 5, 6 or 7 for a moment and a half. The full battle log with both scores is printed to the Serial Monitor, and the first player to reach three points wins the match before a new one starts.
ADVANTAGES:
- Real buttons make the game playable without a computer terminal, while Serial keeps the score.
- Random CPU moves mean no pattern to memorise, so every match feels fresh.
- Dedicated win / lose / draw LEDs give instant visual feedback.
- Running score and match reset are handled automatically by the program.
- Uses only an Uno, three buttons and three LEDs - no extra modules.
REAL-WORLD USES:
- Quick decision-making tools such as picking who goes first.
- Classroom demos of comparison logic and random number generation.
- Entertainment modules inside handheld games and arcade machines.
- Examples of judging algorithms and scoring systems.
- Party gadgets that settle small disputes automatically.
WHAT YOU LEARN:
- How to convert button presses into a numbered choice.
- How random() produces a computer-controlled opponent.
- How to encode game rules inside a small judging function.
- How to keep and display two running scores.
- How to structure a match with a "first to N points" rule.
FUTURE SCOPE: Multiple difficulty levels, a longer best-of-seven format or a match history log could all be added without changing the basic wiring.
POSSIBLE UPGRADES:
- Add a buzzer that plays a different sound for win, lose and draw.
- Show the score on an LCD or seven-segment display.
- Print the CPU's move symbols in ASCII art for a clearer battle screen.
- Count how many matches each side has won overall.
REQUIRED COMPONENTS:
- Arduino Uno
- Breadboard
- 3 push buttons
- 3 LEDs (any colors)
- 3 x 220 ohm resistors
- Jumper wires
- USB cable
- Library: None
