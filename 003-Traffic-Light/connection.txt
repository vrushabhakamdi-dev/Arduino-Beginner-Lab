CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

RED LED
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D2
K (-) → Arduino GND

YELLOW LED
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D3
K (-) → Arduino GND

GREEN LED
A (+) → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D4
K (-) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- One 220 ohm resistor per LED; cathode (short leg) goes to GND.
- Only one light is lit at a time, so current draw stays low.
- Serial Monitor at 9600 baud prints the active light state.
