CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

PUSH BUTTON - START / STOP
Leg 1 → Arduino D2
Leg 2 → GND

PUSH BUTTON - RESET
Leg 1 → Arduino D3
Leg 2 → GND

NOTES
- Both buttons use the internal pull-up: no external resistors needed.
- Pressed reads LOW, released reads HIGH.
- Breadboard, jumper wires and USB cable; no other power is required.
