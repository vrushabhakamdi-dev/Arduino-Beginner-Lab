CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

PUSH BUTTON (tactile)
leg 1 → Arduino D2
leg 2 → Arduino GND

NOTES
- INPUT_PULLUP is used in the code, so NO external resistor is needed.
- Active-low input: pressed = LOW, released = HIGH.
- A 4-pin tactile switch has two legs per side; use one leg from each side.
