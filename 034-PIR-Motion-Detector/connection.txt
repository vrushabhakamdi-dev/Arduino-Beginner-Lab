CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

HC-SR501 PIR MOTION SENSOR MODULE
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
OUT → Arduino D2
  OUT = motion flag, HIGH while movement is detected

BUILT-IN LED (on the Arduino Uno)
LED → Arduino D13
  on-board indicator LED, ON while motion is detected

NOTES
- Warm-up: leave the sensor still for 30-60 seconds after power-up before judging results - the sketch prints a notice after 45 s.
- Left potentiometer = sensitivity (turn clockwise for longer detection distance); right potentiometer = how long OUT stays HIGH after motion stops.
- Set the jumper on the back to L (re-triggering) so OUT stays HIGH while movement continues; H is non-retriggering.
- No external LED or resistor is required - the indicator is the built-in LED on D13.
- Use jumper wires; upload with the USB cable and watch the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
