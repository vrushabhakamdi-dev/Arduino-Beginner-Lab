CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

JOYSTICK MODULE
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
VRx → Arduino A0     horizontal (left/right) position, 0..1023
VRy → Arduino A1     vertical (up/down) position, 0..1023
SW  → Arduino D2     push button, press shorts D2 to GND

LED 1 → Arduino D3     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 2 → Arduino D4     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 3 → Arduino D5     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 4 → Arduino D6     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 5 → Arduino D7     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 6 → Arduino D8     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 7 → Arduino D9     220R + LED, cathode to GND
LED 8 → Arduino D10    220R + LED, cathode to GND

LED LEGS (same for all eight)
Arduino pin → 220R → LED + (anode, long leg)
LED - (cathode, short leg) → Arduino GND

NOTES
- SW uses the internal pull-up (INPUT_PULLUP): wire SW to D2 and the other
  side of the switch to GND. No external resistor needed. LOW = pressed.
- The VRx/VRy pins are analogue outputs; do not move them off A0/A1.
- Each LED needs its own 220 ohm resistor. Reversing an LED (cathode to the
  pin) simply leaves it dark.
- All of D2..D10 are used; D11..D13 and A2..A5 stay free.
- Breadboard, jumper wires and the USB cable complete the build.
