CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

KY-040 ROTARY ENCODER MODULE
CLK → Arduino D2
  CLK = quadrature channel A, a change on it marks one click of the knob
DT  → Arduino D3
  DT  = quadrature channel B, its level compared with CLK gives the direction
SW  → Arduino D4
  SW  = push button on top of the knob, active LOW, internal pull-up
+   → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND

NOTES
- All three signal pins use the internal pull-up resistors (INPUT_PULLUP), so no external resistors are needed.
- Press the knob to reset the position to 0: SW reads LOW while pressed, HIGH when released.
- If the count runs the wrong way, swap the CLK and DT wires (or swap the two branches in the code).
- No library is required; build on a breadboard with jumper wires, upload with the USB cable and watch the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
