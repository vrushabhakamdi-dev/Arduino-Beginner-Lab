CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

POTENTIOMETER (10k)
Left outer leg  → Arduino 5V
Right outer leg → Arduino GND
Wiper (middle)  → Arduino A0
  Turning the knob sweeps the frequency from 100 Hz to 5000 Hz.

PUSH BUTTON - TONE ON / OFF
One leg   → Arduino D2
Other leg → GND
  Press once to mute, press again to sound the tone.

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
- → GND
  Outputs the generated square-wave tone.

NOTES
- The potentiometer must be wired across 5V and GND with its wiper to A0.
  Reversing the outer legs simply reverses the pitch direction.
- tone() only produces a SQUARE wave - that is the normal Arduino limit, not a
  wiring fault. An active buzzer will ignore the frequency and hum at its own
  fixed pitch, so a PASSIVE buzzer is required.
- Button uses the internal pull-up: wire it straight between D2 and GND.
- Current status and frequency are printed in the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.
