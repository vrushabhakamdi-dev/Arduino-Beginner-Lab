CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

MQ-2 GAS SENSOR MODULE
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
AO  → Arduino A0    analog output - gas level, 0 to 1023
DO  → not connected (digital output not used by this sketch)

PIEZO BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
- → Arduino GND

RED LED
+ → 220 ohm resistor → Arduino D6
- → Arduino GND

NOTES
- WARM-UP: the alarm is disabled for the first 60 seconds after power-up (WARMUP_MS); readings before that are meaningless.
- A brand new MQ-2 needs 24 to 48 hours of continuous power to burn in, and 1-2 minutes of warm-up after every power cycle.
- The internal heater makes the mesh hot - never touch it while powered.
- Fresh air on a new sensor usually reads about 100-300; the alarm trips at GAS_THRESHOLD = 500. Watch your own baseline in Serial and adjust.
- Only AO is wired; DO and its onboard trimmer are unused.
- Educational prototype only - not a certified gas alarm.
