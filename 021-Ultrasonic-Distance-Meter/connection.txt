CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

HC-SR04 ULTRASONIC SENSOR
VCC  → Arduino 5V
GND  → Arduino GND
TRIG → Arduino D9     output - the 10 us trigger pulse is sent from here
ECHO → Arduino D10    input - the echo pulse length is measured on here

NOTES
- TRIG is an OUTPUT, ECHO is an INPUT. Swapping them gives "Out of range (no echo)" every time.
- Power from 5V only; the module does not work reliably on 3.3V.
- Readings are only trusted between 2 cm and 400 cm - outside that the sketch prints "Out of range".
- pulseIn() waits a maximum of 30 ms, so "no echo" means nothing in range, a soft/angled surface, or a loose wire.
