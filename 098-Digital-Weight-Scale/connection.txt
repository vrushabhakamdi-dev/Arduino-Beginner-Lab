CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

HX711 LOAD CELL AMPLIFIER
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
DT  → Arduino D4    data out
SCK → Arduino D5    clock

LOAD CELL (4-WIRE STRAIN GAUGE) → HX711

  LOAD CELL    HX711
  red    ------→ E+
  black  ------→ E-
  white  ------→ A-
  green  ------→ A+

  All four cell wires go straight to the HX711 screw/pad terminals.

PUSH BUTTON - TARE
leg 1 → Arduino D2
leg 2 → Arduino GND

1602 LCD WITH I2C BACKPACK (PCF8574, ADDR 0x27)
VCC → Arduino 5V
GND → Arduino GND
SDA → Arduino A4
SCL → Arduino A5

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE / KNOWN CALIBRATION WEIGHT
no pin mapping - used for prototyping, power and calibration

NOTES
- The HX711 runs from 5 V; keep DT/SCK and the load-cell wires short and
  away from motor or mains wiring so readings stay steady.
- Load cell colours vary between makers - follow your cell's diagram. A
  negative reading only means two wires are swapped.
- Calibrate once: tare empty, weigh a known load, then
  NEW = OLD * (shown / known) in CALIBRATION_FACTOR.
- If the LCD/Serial says NO HX711, re-check DT -> D4 and SCK -> D5.
- A4/A5 stay free for the LCD - the HX711 does not use I2C here.
