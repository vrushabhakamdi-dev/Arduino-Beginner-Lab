CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

LED
Anode (+) → Arduino D13     (through 220 ohm resistor)
Cathode (−) → GND

PUSH BUTTON
Leg 1 → Arduino D2
Leg 2 → GND
  Internal pull-up is used: HIGH at rest, LOW while pressed.

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
− → GND

RANDOM SEED PIN
A0 → not connected (leave floating)
  Read once at start-up so random() gets a fresh seed on every reset.

NOTES
- One 220 ohm resistor in series with the LED.
- No external supply: the USB cable powers the Uno.
- Breadboard and jumper wires make up the rest of the circuit.
