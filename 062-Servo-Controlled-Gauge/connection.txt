CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

10K POTENTIOMETER
left leg   → Arduino 5V
wiper      → Arduino A0     gauge input (0..1023)
right leg  → Arduino GND

SG90 MICRO SERVO
+ (red)         → Arduino 5V
- (brown/black) → Arduino GND
S (orange)      → Arduino D9     PWM needle signal

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE
no pin mapping - used for prototyping and power

NOTES
- Only the middle leg (wiper) of the pot goes to A0; the two outer legs
  are 5V and GND (they can be swapped).
- The two outer legs of the pot and the servo share the Uno 5V/GND rail.
- Servo signal must be on D9, the sketch does gaugeNeedle.attach(9).
