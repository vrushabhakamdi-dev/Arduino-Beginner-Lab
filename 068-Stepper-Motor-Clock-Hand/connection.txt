CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

ULN2003 STEPPER DRIVER
IN1  → Arduino D8     28BYJ-48 coil sequence
IN2  → Arduino D9     28BYJ-48 coil sequence
IN3  → Arduino D10    28BYJ-48 coil sequence
IN4  → Arduino D11    28BYJ-48 coil sequence
VCC  → Arduino 5V
GND  → Arduino GND

28BYJ-48 STEPPER MOTOR
5-pin plug → ULN2003 board motor connector ( plugs straight in )

BREADBOARD / JUMPER WIRES / USB CABLE
no pin mapping - used for prototyping and power

NOTES
- IN1..IN4 order is fixed by the code: D8=IN1, D9=IN2, D10=IN3, D11=IN4.
- The ULN2003 board is powered from the Arduino 5V pin; no external
  supply is needed for a 28BYJ-48.
- No buttons or sensors in this build - timing comes from millis().
