CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

POTENTIOMETER (10 kOhm) - time selector
Outer pin 1 → 5V
Outer pin 2 → GND
Wiper (middle pin) → Arduino A0     (selects 10 to 120 seconds)

PUSH BUTTON - START / CANCEL
Leg 1 → Arduino D2
Leg 2 → GND

LED
Anode (+) → Arduino D13     (through 220 ohm resistor)
Cathode (−) → GND

PASSIVE BUZZER
+ → Arduino D8
− → GND

NOTES
- Button uses the internal pull-up, so no external resistor is needed.
- One 220 ohm resistor in series with the LED.
- USB cable powers everything; breadboard and jumper wires for the connections.
