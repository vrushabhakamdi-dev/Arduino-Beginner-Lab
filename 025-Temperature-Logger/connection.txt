CONNECTIONS

BOARD: Arduino Uno

TMP36 TEMPERATURE SENSOR
(flat face toward you, pins left to right: Vout, Vs, GND)
Vout → Arduino A0    analog output - 0.5 V at 0 C, +10 mV per degree C
Vs   → Arduino 5V
GND  → Arduino GND

NOTES
- Orientation matters: flat face toward you, left pin = Vout (to A0), middle = Vs (to 5V), right = GND. Reversing Vs and GND can destroy the sensor.
- Warm-up: wait about 30 seconds after power-up before trusting the numbers.
- Do not touch the sensor body - body heat raises the reading by several degrees.
- One CSV row (millis,degrees_c,degrees_f) is printed every 2 seconds at 9600 baud.
